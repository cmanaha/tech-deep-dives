### G5. Two Daly-Young assumptions for Calculator D, not one (section 18, W4-19)
- 2410.21680v2 Appendix A names the assumptions behind dt*: "(dt >> u0, wcp, q and R >> u0, q, dt_cp, wcp)". Sec III gives the regime checked against Monte Carlo: "(Nnodes rf)^-1 >~ 0.1 day ... accurate to within ~5%".
