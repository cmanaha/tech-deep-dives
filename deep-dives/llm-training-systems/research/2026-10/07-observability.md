**T07-2. - Location: pytorch/torchtitan v0.4.0, `torchtitan/observability/metrics.py` lines 115 to 135 (`compute_training_performance_metrics`); `torchtitan/tools/utils.py` lines 111 to 156 (`get_peak_flops`).

### B. Hangs and timeouts: the PyTorch NCCL watchdog and flight recorder

**T07-5. A collective on the NCCL process group times out after 10 minutes by default (30 minutes for other backends). At timeout PyTorch aborts collectives asynchronously and crashes the process rather than continuing, because CUDA execution is asynchronous and later kernels could run on corrupted data.**
- Location: pytorch v2.15.0, `torch/csrc/distributed/c10d/ProcessGroupNCCL.hpp` lines 164 to 165; `torch/csrc/distributed/c10d/ProcessGroup.hpp` lines 25 to 26; `torch/distributed/distributed_c10d.py` lines 2515 to 2520 (`init_process_group` docstring).
- Verdict: NEW
- Evidence: https://github.com/pytorch/pytorch/blob/31a77fc9c0a7c6fff15a1ff58c743f53b1a9d457/torch/csrc/distributed/c10d/ProcessGroupNCCL.hpp | Tier 1, code at pinned tag | default value | "constexpr auto kProcessGroupNCCLDefaultTimeout = std::chrono::milliseconds(10 * 60 * 1000);". https://github.com/pytorch/pytorch/blob/31a77fc9c0a7c6fff15a1ff58c743f53b1a9d457/torch/csrc/distributed/c10d/ProcessGroup.hpp | "constexpr auto kProcessGroupDefaultTimeout = std::chrono::milliseconds(30 * 60 * 1000);". https://github.com/pytorch/pytorch/blob/31a77fc9c0a7c6fff15a1ff58c743f53b1a9d457/torch/distributed/distributed_c10d.py | "timeout (timedelta, optional): Timeout for operations executed against the process group. - Use in dive: practical. The timeout is a trade: long enough to survive a slow checkpoint save or a link flap (register 04 T04-16, MegaScale raised it for NIC flapping), short enough that a dead rank does not idle the cluster. Anything that blocks all ranks longer than 10 minutes between collectives (a synchronous checkpoint to slow storage, first-step compilation) needs either a larger `timeout=` or to finish inside it.
- Link: register 04 T04-16.
