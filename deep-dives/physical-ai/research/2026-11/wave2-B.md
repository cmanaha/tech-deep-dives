# Wave 2 refutation and freshness: registers 04, 05, 06

**Date:** 2026-11-20. **Agent:** W2-B. **Registers:** `04-rl-and-agentic-loops.md`, `05-data-pipeline.md`, `06-simulation-and-sim-to-real.md`. **Sources fetched:** 201 of the 204 distinct URLs the three registers name (two bare raw-host directory prefixes return 404 by design; one gated dataset card returns 401 at the cited path and was read at its public `/resolve/` path), plus 31 further pinned files and pages the registers cite by path or that were needed to test a claim, plus about 70 read-only GitHub, PyPI and arXiv API calls for currency.

## Method and coverage

- Every URL in the three registers was re-fetched on 2026-11-20 into a fresh directory under `/tmp`: arXiv PDFs at the pinned version (converted with `pdftotext`, plain and layout modes), GitHub files at the pinned 40-character SHA or tag, first-party pages as served.
- A script searched for every quoted string of 25 characters or more in the text of the entry's own sources (whitespace, hyphenation and symbol normalisation only), and checked that every multi-digit number in the claim headline occurs in those sources. Rows tagged `[script]` rest on that: the quote is verbatim in the pinned source and the headline numbers are present; the surrounding paragraph was not re-read by hand. Rows tagged `[hand]` were read, parsed or recomputed individually. Rows tagged `[synthesis]` are the registers' own cross-entry summaries; their inputs are checked in their own rows.
- Hand-checked rows: 22 in register 04, 44 in register 05, 25 in register 06. These cover every priority item in the task: all ENPIRE figures, ASPIRE v2 and arXiv 2608.07555v2 figures, the Recap robot count, the SO-101 RL paths, the AWS sample at its pinned SHA, the LeRobot v0.7.0 format and codec defaults, every NVIDIA encode/decode matrix row, the EC2 vCPU and G7 rows, the Cosmos Curator attribution, the Cosmos 3 timings, the 32 H100 run, the Isaac Lab Mimic figures, every `[derived]` bytes-per-hour and cores-per-GPU figure, the simulator versions, the Isaac Sim and Isaac Lab hardware sentences, the MuJoCo Warp throughput attribution, the RT core counts, the correlation figures and the confidence-interval arithmetic.
- Every entry of the three registers has a row (87, 108, 102). What was skipped: for `[script]` rows, the "Use in dive" prose and its secondary quotes were checked for verbatim presence only, not for whether the paraphrase around them is fair; section 5 (teaching material), section 7 (mental models) and section 10 (reading path) of each register were not checked; the Newton `sensors.rst` quote at commit 9bd0215d in F06-62 and the SimReady, Genesis and LeIsaac items the registers themselves mark unread were not fetched.
- Currency: `gh api` release and tag listings for 27 repositories, PyPI JSON for 10 packages, and one arXiv API query covering all 39 paper IDs.
