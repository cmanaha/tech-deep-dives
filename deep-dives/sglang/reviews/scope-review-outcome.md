## Verdict

**What the plan gets right.** The mechanism layer is strong: "admission is a bet on early stopping", "the backend picks the page size", "the gateway remembers where it sent text", "PD is a rendezvous, then a push" and "on EFA, KV transfer and expert all-to-all are two stacks" each predict behaviour a reader has not yet seen, and each comes with the log line or knob that shows it. Hedges are preserved almost everywhere (Trainium, the DLC lines, benchmark claims, code read but not run), the benchmark section refuses to compute a winner, and the AWS layer covers the path choice, both DLC lines, EFA for PD and for EP, and Trainium status with the right scope.

**What keeps it from the outcome.** The plan is organised backward, from SGLang's internals, for a reader who arrives forward, from vLLM. The headline model leads with a property vLLM shares (#1) and over-claims what orbits it (#2); the remaining-differences list misses the scheduler defaults (#3); the "why" against vLLM sits in a section on no reading route (#5, #20); and the reader's first two moments, a first launch and a port from vLLM, have no section of their own (#4, #19). On AWS, the most likely SageMaker failure is split across two sections that never meet (#10), the key advice contradicts itself (#11), the P6 page-size fact has no action (#9), instance choice is mapped but not answered (#14), and cost is absent though every SGLang-specific cost lever is already sourced (#13).

**Scores (1 to 10).** Mental model: 6. Practical leverage: 7. AWS fit: 7. The fixes are mostly reorders, joins and one-line additions from rows already in the registers; only #13's price figures and #15's worked example need new sourcing. With #1 to #5, #9 to #11 and #13 applied, all three scores should reach 8 or above.
