## Verdict

74 findings: 25 wrong, incomplete or missing row IDs; 29 dropped conditions; 7 strengthened hedges or claims; 9 unsupported claims or unlabelled syntheses; 4 register conflicts. The scope's facts are mostly right. Its evidence pointers are not: about a third of the IDs, the thesis's included, point at the neighbouring row or a row on another subject, so a fact-checker following them would reject true claims. The repeated substantive failure is lost conditions. "Read from code, not run", "code-derived", "AWS sample claim", "not located" and workload scopes (prefill-only scoring, SM90+ FP8 shapes, no topk>1 speculation) fall away between register and scope, and rule 14 asks that each survive. Three findings change what a reader would do and must be fixed before drafting. Finding 21: KV sizing divides by a per-layer figure, which overstates capacity by the layer count. Finding 50: the admin-plane exposure claim drops the 127.0.0.1 default bind. Finding 62: the CVE count drops that none of the five critical CVEs is in the default LLM serving path. Finding 39 (vLLM's "default pull") and finding 43 (the gateway contrast, where vLLM's router is a fork of SGLang's) misstate the vLLM comparison, which is the dive's headline. Register-side fixes are needed in 07 G3 and mental model 4, 08 mental model 2, 09 E3, register 02's section recommendation and register 00's storage label. Once the row IDs are corrected and the conditions restored, the scope is grounded. None of the findings calls for new research.
