## Tier definitions

- **Tier 0** — Our own experiments. Code, data, and inputs checked into `research/` or `iac/`.
- **Tier 1** — Official vendor documentation, formal specifications, source code.
- **Tier 2** — Vendor blog posts, conference talks (re:Invent, HotChips, MICRO, ISCA), official whitepapers.
- **Tier 3** — Peer-reviewed papers, third-party technical analysis with methodology disclosed.
- **Tier 4** — Blog posts and tutorials. Inspiration only. Never cited as source of truth.
