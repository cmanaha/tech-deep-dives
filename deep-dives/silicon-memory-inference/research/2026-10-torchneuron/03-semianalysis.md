### How the verdicts were applied

- CONFIRMED-1P: a first-party source states the fact; the dive cites that source, never SemiAnalysis. Where a row's evidence also shows a SemiAnalysis-only detail, the "How the dive may use it" column says which part is 1P and which part stays attributed.
- SA-ONLY: SemiAnalysis is the origin (its own measurements, its access to AWS plans or internals); nothing first-party contradicts it. Tier 3, attributed only.
- UNVERIFIABLE: a claim about a third party or a public fact where a first-party source should exist but none was located (or the cited source could not be fetched without logging in).
- CONTRADICTED: a first-party source or pinned code says otherwise; both sides quoted.
- OPINION: evaluative; the row says whether it passes the dive's content test.
