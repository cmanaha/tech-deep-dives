## 5. `sections/DeterminismAOT.tsx` (+ `components/DeterminismDiagram.tsx`)

**What the section covers.** Per-call reproducibility as a regulatory and operational property. Trainium is presented as "deterministic by construction" because the NEFF (Neuron Executable File Format) binary fixes the schedule. NVIDIA GPUs are presented as "deterministic on opt-in" through cuBLAS, cuDNN and CCCL flags. A six-row regulatory table maps frameworks to a determinism hook, and a closing block pairs determinism (temporal isolation) with NIE (Nitro Isolation Engine), MIG and TEE-I/O (spatial isolation).

**Headline.** The section's two-path framing does not survive current primary sources. On both platforms the dominant source of run-to-run difference in a serving system is batch composition, not the accelerator. vLLM and SGLang now ship opt-in batch-invariant modes for exactly this reason [T1-41][T1-42]. vLLM-Neuron picks a different NEFF bucket on every decode step depending on what else is in the batch [T1-45], so "same NEFF + same input" is not something the caller controls. Three of the five GPU bullets in the diagram name controls that do not exist in the form stated (CUDA "deterministic mode", cuBLAS "deterministic mode", CCCL 3.1 "collective determinism"). SR 11-7, the first row of the regulatory table, was superseded on 2026-04-17 by SR 26-2, which puts generative AI out of scope [T1-43].

**What the section covers.** Small language models (SLMs, roughly 0.1B to 14B parameters): a 14-model parameter and context chart, four reasons SLMs matter (cost, latency, on-device, drafting), four deployment patterns (single GPU, MIG, host CPU, drafter), an arithmetic-intensity argument that SLM weights fit in CPU cache, and limits of SLMs.

**Headline.** The chart is a late-2024 lineup. Every family in it has shipped a successor since, and context windows moved from 8K-32K to 128K-262K: Qwen3.5 (0.8B to 9B, 262,144 tokens natively, hybrid Gated DeltaNet plus attention) [T1-60], Gemma 4 (E2B and E4B at "2.3B effective" and "4.5B effective", 128K; 12B at 256K) [T1-59], SmolLM3-3B [T1-61], Phi-4-mini [T1-56], Ministral 3 (3B, 8B, 14B, 256K) [T1-62]. Meta has published no newer small Llama on Hugging Face since Llama 3.2 (September 2024) as of this pass [T1-67]. Two of the section's quantitative arguments fail arithmetic against cited hardware figures: a 1B INT8 model (1 GB) does not fit in any host CPU L3 named (largest 512 MB, and an EPYC CCD sees 32 MB of it) [T1-10][T1-11], and batch-1 sub-millisecond decode of a 1B model exceeds a Turin socket's 614 GB/s bandwidth ceiling **[derived]**. The B300 MIG figure ("7 instances of ~34 GB") has no NVIDIA source, a finding the P3 audit (2026-08-01) already made and that still holds.
